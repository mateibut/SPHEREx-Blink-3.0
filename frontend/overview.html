Research Pipeline

How Blink detection works.

01

Select & Acquire

Enter sky coordinates or open a documented target field. Available images depend on the selected data source and backend.

02

Blink & Subtract

Inspect images across survey passes with blink and difference views. Passes may cover different wavelengths.

03

Inspect & export

Review candidate motion and supporting detections, compare with available catalogues, and export results with provenance. A candidate is a lead, not a discovery.
