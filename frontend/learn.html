    Astra is ready. Pick a topic to begin.

    Redshift

    Light waves stretch as space expands. This can shift distant light toward longer, infrared wavelengths.

    Spectral signature

    A pattern in light, like fingerprints, that can give clues about the material around an object.

    Signal-to-noise ratio

    How clearly a signal stands above random background. A strong signal is easier to see, but it is not proof by itself.

    Candidate mover

    A point that seems to change position between images. It is a clue that deserves checking.
